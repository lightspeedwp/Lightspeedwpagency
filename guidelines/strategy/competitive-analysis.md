## AI builders and AI-assisted site platforms

Major platforms market AI-assisted section generation and workflow acceleration; this compresses perceived value of page assembly.
LightSpeed advantage: governance, ownership, and system integrity under scale; AI can accelerate content and drafts, but cannot replace operational accountability.

## Headless consultancies

Offer modern UX and "app-like" interactions, but often at higher complexity and editorial cost. LightSpeed can counter with WordPress-native progressive interactivity and block bindings where appropriate.

## Traditional agencies and offshore delivery

Compete on scope and hourly output; LightSpeed should compete on system deliverables, measurable outcomes, and transfer.

## Key differentiators to communicate

1. **Governed systems vs pages** — Design tokens, pattern governance, editor safety
2. **WordPress-native progressive interactivity** — Interactivity API over JS frameworks
3. **Measurable outcomes** — CWV targets, publishing velocity, conversion instrumentation
4. **Ownership transfer** — Documentation, governance models, training as deliverables
5. **AI search readiness** — Structured content, citation-ready formatting, technical SEO
