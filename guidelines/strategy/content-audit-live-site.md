# Live Site Content Audit

## Page inventory from primary navigation and key links

### Core pages
- Home
- Services hub
- Solutions hub
- About
- Contact
- Portfolio (linked in nav)
- Blog (categories: News, LSX, Content, Design, Process)

### Service detail pages (from Services nav)
Content, Discovery, Design, Development, Migrations, Support, Hosting, Security

### Solution detail pages (from Solutions nav)
WordPress, WooCommerce, Tour Operators, LSX Products

### System/legal pages
Privacy Policy, Terms & Conditions, Policies & Principles (footer)

## Strengths

- Clear service and solution navigation reduces ambiguity for first-time visitors.
- Credible third-party endorsement supports WooCommerce authority.
- "Community" and contribution narrative exists and can be elevated into a differentiator.

## Weaknesses

- Hero messaging is broad and category-generic; it does not state a defensible "system outcome" or a specialised operating model.
- Services are described as agency functions rather than system deliverables and measurable outcomes.
- Publishing and eLearning appear as labels without deep system explanation.

## Redundancies

- Repeated "Why WordPress?" style blocks repeat across the site; they don't add new authority beyond basic platform advocacy.

## Missing authority signals

- No prominent "method" pages explaining token systems, pattern governance, or editorial workflows as deliverables (despite internal maturity in these areas).
- No visible performance budgets or CWV targets.
- No explicit AI search readiness guidance (despite it being a 2026 buying concern).

## CTA analysis

- Primary CTAs focus on "Free consultation" and "View our work."
- Missing a mid-funnel CTA for qualified leads: "Get a systems audit" / "Request architecture review" / "See pattern governance model."

## Scannability and structure issues

- Key pages use repeated blocks and long-form paragraphs without a consistent "problem -> system -> outcome" scannability pattern.

## Structural SEO issues

- Site taxonomy is service-led rather than entity-led; this weakens topic cluster depth for AI-style retrieval where foundational SEO still applies.

## Internal linking weaknesses

- Services and solutions are siloed; there is limited systematic cross-linking from service claims to proof pages (case studies) and to system methodology pages.
