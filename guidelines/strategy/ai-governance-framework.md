# AI Governance Framework

## Purpose

AI is used to accelerate drafting, analysis, and operations, but governance protects brand, legal, and editorial integrity.

## Representation rules

### No invisible automation
Any AI-derived public claim must be reviewable and traceable to a source.

### Search inclusion awareness
Google AI features follow standard Search eligibility; therefore technical SEO and content quality standards remain the baseline.

### Crawl policy decisions
Decide whether to allow OAI-Searchbot crawl for ChatGPT search inclusion; document the decision and implement at CDN/robots level accordingly.

## Content risk tiers

### Low risk
Style rewrites, outline suggestions, internal ideation.

### Medium risk
Strategic recommendations, comparative claims.

### High risk
Medical, legal, financial advice (should be avoided or heavily reviewed), claims about compliance.

## Review gates

### Editorial review
Accuracy, tone, proof integrity.

### Legal/compliance review
Accessibility and regulatory content; note EAA applicability signals for e-commerce and other services from June 28, 2025.

### Engineering review
Performance impact and technical correctness.
