## ICP definition

### Publishing systems lead
**Role:** Head of Content/Editorial Ops, Managing Editor, Digital Director.
**Jobs to be done:** speed up publishing, reduce production errors, keep templates consistent, maintain performance, improve distribution across search and AI summaries.

### Commerce systems owner
**Role:** eCommerce Manager, Head of Growth, Ops lead.
**Jobs to be done:** improve conversion, stabilise checkout, reduce platform risk, keep governance while scaling product/catalog complexity.

### Platform and governance buyer
**Role:** CTO, Head of Engineering, Digital Transformation lead.
**Jobs to be done:** reduce incidents, standardise delivery across brands, enforce accessibility and compliance, ensure maintainable ownership transfer.

## Messaging framework

### Core problem statement
Most WordPress sites fail at scale because design systems, content operations, and performance governance are treated as optional.

### Desired transformation
From "pages" to a governed publishing system: tokenised design, curated patterns, measurable distribution, and safe editorial autonomy.

### Objection handling
**"We can build this faster with an AI builder."**
Response: AI system outputs pages; LightSpeed outputs a governed system that stays coherent under scale and compliance, and remains eligible for search and AI features.

### Proof strategy
Lead with platform credibility (community, partners), then with quantified outcomes (performance, velocity, conversion), then with method deliverables (tokens, patterns, governance).

## AI and AEO optimisation framework

### FAQ architecture
Use FAQs for user comprehension and AI retrieval, not as a rich result tactic; Google limits FAQ rich results to specific authoritative domains.
Create "FAQ blocks" as patterns with consistent question/answer layout and internal linking to deeper definitional pages.

### Schema strategy
- Article schema for Insights posts and case studies; include author identity and consistent profiles.
- Paywalled markup only where applicable (membership/publishing clients).

### Entity reinforcement
Create definitional pages and ensure repeated "entity phrases" in headings and first paragraphs.

### Citation-ready formatting
Answer-first sections; short paragraphs; clear sourcing; consistent claims-to-proof linking.
Align with Google's guidance that AI features follow foundational SEO and technical eligibility requirements.

### Semantic linking model
Each Systems page links to: one proof case study, one playbook, and two supporting articles.
