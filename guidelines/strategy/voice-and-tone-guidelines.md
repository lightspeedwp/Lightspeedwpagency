# Voice and Tone Guidelines

## Voice principles

### Systems-first clarity
Explain what the system is and what it unlocks, then show proof.

### Decisive and practical
Use strong opinions, avoid vague agency language, and prioritise implementation details.

### Measurable outcomes
Tie claims to metrics, process artefacts, and governance deliverables.

## Tone spectrum

### Technical mode
Used in Systems and Engineering pages. Precise, constraint-driven, implementation-ready.

### Advisory mode
Used in Solutions pages. Outcome framing, decision guidance, risk management.

### Founder mode
Used in Essays / selected Insights. First-person perspective allowed, but always tethered to an operational lesson.

## What to avoid

- Generic agency phrases without system specificity.
- Tool worship (AI is a tool; governance is the product).

## How to write for AI search

- Use definitional headers and answer-first formatting.
- Prefer short, scannable sections; use clear terminology consistently across pages.

## Formatting rules for scannability

- Use meaningful headings; keep paragraphs short.
- Use "What / Why / How / Proof" blocks for system pages.

## Authority positioning style

- Lead with method artefacts: token system, pattern library, governance.
- Support with evidence: CWV targets, measured results, partner proof.
