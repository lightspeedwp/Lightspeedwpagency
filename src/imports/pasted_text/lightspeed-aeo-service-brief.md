# **LightSpeed AI Engine Optimisation (AEO) Service Brief**

### **🔹 Summary**

* **Value** — The AI Engine Optimisation service page positions LightSpeed as an authority in making brands discoverable by AI models. It covers structured data enhancement, authoritative signals and AI visibility tracking, outlines a four‑step process and lists expected results such as increased AI citations and improved accuracy[\[1\]](https://lightspeedwp.figma.site/services/ai/engine-optimisation).  
    
* **Risks** — The page lacks immersive visuals; static cards and minimal metrics do not convey the complexity of AEO. Without examples, visitors may not understand how entity mapping, content restructuring or citation tracking work. The “Ignite” phase label may confuse users without context.  
    
* **Next Step** — Develop a page that visually narrates how AI engines interpret and rank content. Use WebGL to depict structured data and entity graphs, animate the four‑step process, and provide evidence of impact.

### **1\. Core Content Audit**

1. **Hero** — Title *“AI Engine Optimization (AEO)”* with subheading emphasising positioning your brand for the AI‑powered future of search, plus CTAs for an AI visibility audit and a link to the parent service. The hero uses a simple grid background and highlights the “Ignite” phase[\[2\]](https://lightspeedwp.figma.site/services/ai/engine-optimisation).  
     
2. **Features** — Three cards: Structured data enhancement (schema markup implementation), Authoritative signals (building E‑E‑A‑T signals like experience, expertise, authoritativeness, trust), AI visibility tracking (monitoring brand presence across AI‑generated responses)[\[1\]](https://lightspeedwp.figma.site/services/ai/engine-optimisation).  
     
3. **Process** — Four steps:  
     
4. **AI Audit** — Analyse how AI engines understand and reference your brand; deliverables: AI citation audit, entity relationship mapping, content structure analysis.  
     
5. **Strategy Development** — Create a roadmap for improving AI visibility; deliverables: AEO strategy document, priority content targets, implementation timeline[\[3\]](https://lightspeedwp.figma.site/services/ai/engine-optimisation).  
     
6. **Content Optimisation** — Enhance content for AI understanding and citation; deliverables: Content restructuring, schema implementation, authority building[\[3\]](https://lightspeedwp.figma.site/services/ai/engine-optimisation).  
     
7. **Monitoring & Refinement** — Track AI visibility and refine strategy; deliverables: Monthly AI visibility reports, citation tracking, strategy adjustments[\[3\]](https://lightspeedwp.figma.site/services/ai/engine-optimisation).  
     
8. **Expected Results** — Four metrics: Increase in AI citations, More AI‑generated referrals, Accuracy of AI brand info, Time to significant impact[\[4\]](https://lightspeedwp.figma.site/services/ai/engine-optimisation).  
     
9. **CTA** — Invitation to request a free AI visibility audit.

### **2\. Recommended WebGL & Interaction Plan**

1. **Entity Graph Hero** — Visualise the AI engine as a network of entities. Use WebGL to display your brand in the centre with connecting nodes representing products, authors and topics. Lines pulse as the user scrolls, showing how structured data clarifies relationships. Hovering on a node reveals a tooltip explaining the entity and its role in AEO.  
     
2. **Four‑Step Journey Animation** — Represent the process with a particle moving through four 3D portals. Each portal corresponds to one of the steps (Audit → Strategy → Optimisation → Monitoring). As the particle passes through, icons and bullet deliverables appear. Provide controls to play/pause or step through each phase manually.  
     
3. **AEO Impact Dashboard** — Create interactive gauges or counters for the four expected results. These could fill to show increases in AI citations and referrals. Use micro‑animations to highlight improvements over time.

### **3\. Content Enhancement Actions**

1. **Add Detailed Examples** — Illustrate how structured data markup increases AI citations. Provide a before/after snippet or a mini case study. Explain entity mapping and content restructuring using short diagrams or code snippets.  
     
2. **Explain the “Ignite” Phase** — Clarify the lifecycle context by linking the Ignite phase to the six‑stage service model. Show what happens after Ignite (e.g., Create, Build, Launch) and how AEO fits into ongoing optimisation.  
     
3. **Expand Metrics** — Provide actual numeric benchmarks for the expected results (e.g., “3× increase in AI citations within 4 months”, “90% accuracy of brand information across AI responses”). Back these with internal data.

### **4\. Notes & Considerations**

* Use neon cyan and pink accents for the hero to evoke AI energy and unify with the AI engine optimisation identity.  
    
* Provide static SVG fallback for non‑WebGL devices. Use alt text and ARIA roles for entity graph nodes.  
    
* Offer a CTA linking to more technical resources on schema markup, entity SEO and AI search readiness.
