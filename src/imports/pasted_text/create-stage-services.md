# **Services – Create Stage**

### **Summary**

* **Value** – Converts insights from discovery into tangible designs, content strategies and prototypes. This stage delivers accessible, AI‑ready design systems, Figma prototypes and structured content libraries that lay the groundwork for development.  
    
* **Risks** – Poorly defined designs or unstructured content can lead to rework during development. Without accessibility and AI considerations, the site may be inflexible or excluded from AI search results.  
    
* **Next step** – Align the create stage with AI‑enabled content operations and design system best practices; emphasise accessible, modular patterns and AI‑optimised copy.

### **Content audit**

* **Hero messaging** – The page opens with the tagline **“Create. Design. Prototype. Inspire.”** and explains that this stage turns wireframes into high‑fidelity prototypes and accessible UI, with a typical duration of **3–6 weeks**[\[1\]](https://lightspeedwp.figma.site/services/create). Update the copy to highlight how design systems and prototypes are **AI‑ready**, ensuring content structure and semantics are optimised for AI indexing.  
    
* **Deliverables** – The current page promises wireframes for mobile & desktop, Figma mockups, clickable prototypes, content library (organised copy/images/media) and design tokens[\[2\]](https://lightspeedwp.figma.site/services/create). Expand this list to include **AI‑optimised copy guidelines** and **structured metadata for AI engines**. Use a checklist or grid to present deliverables clearly.  
    
* **Client role** – Clients are expected to review wireframes, supply high‑resolution brand assets, approve final designs and provide page copy[\[2\]](https://lightspeedwp.figma.site/services/create). Clarify that clients should also collaborate on AI prompt guidelines and provide tone‑of‑voice inputs to train AI tools.  
    
* **Services in this phase** – The site currently lists Web Design (mobile‑first accessible UI), Figma Prototyping & Design Systems, and Content Operations[\[3\]](https://lightspeedwp.figma.site/services/create). Merge these into three service cards that emphasise AI: **Accessible UI/UX design**, **Design systems & prototyping**, **Content & AI operations**. Each card should link to a detailed explanation of how AI supports rapid prototyping and content management.  
    
* **Testimonials and CTA** – A testimonial highlights the value of the Figma prototype[\[4\]](https://lightspeedwp.figma.site/services/create). The CTA “Let’s Create Something Beautiful” lists deliverables and invites users to start their design[\[5\]](https://lightspeedwp.figma.site/services/create). Update these to emphasise AI‑optimised designs and prototypes.

### **WebGL hero & interactive ideas**

* **Hero concept** – Visualise creation as a morphing 3D canvas: a set of wireframe cubes and sheets that gradually unfold into colourful, polished blocks, symbolising the journey from wireframe to high‑fidelity design. Use neon‑pink (creativity) and neon‑cyan highlights to animate line drawing, with subtle pulsing to indicate AI energy.  
    
* **Prototype showcase** – Below the hero, embed an interactive slider of mini‑prototypes. Each slide shows a simplified page layout with hotspots; hovering over a hotspot reveals a tooltip describing AI‑enhanced features (e.g., auto‑generated copy suggestions, design token usage). Use WebGL instanced geometry to animate these slide transitions.  
    
* **Design token visualiser** – Offer an expandable visualiser that displays the colour palette, typography scale and spacing tokens used in the design system. Users can hover to see token values and learn how tokens feed into AI‑assisted styling.

### **Recommended actions**

1. **Revise messaging** to clarify that the Create stage produces AI‑optimised design systems and prototypes, not just visuals. Describe how designs and content are structured for AI ingestion and search visibility.  
     
2. **Highlight deliverables** in a grid or checklist: wireframes, prototypes, content library, design tokens, AI copy guidelines, metadata specification.  
     
3. **Incorporate an interactive design token visualiser** so visitors can see how variables like colours, typography and spacing create coherence across the site.  
     
4. **Introduce an AI prompt workshop** as an optional add‑on for clients wanting to train internal AI tools to generate on‑brand copy and images.  
     
5. **Include a stronger testimonial** emphasising how an interactive Figma prototype and structured content accelerated project approval and development.
