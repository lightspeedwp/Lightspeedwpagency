# **Answer Engine Optimisation (AEO) Service Brief**

### **🔹 Summary**

* **Value** — The Answer Engine Optimisation page positions LightSpeed as a partner for winning featured snippets, voice search and AI‑generated answers. It explains why AEO matters, details a four‑phase process and lists expected results (400% increase in snippets, 3× voice search visibility, 85% answer accuracy rate, 2–4 month time to first results)[\[1\]\[2\]](https://lightspeedwp.figma.site/services/ai/answer-engine-optimisation).  
    
* **Risks** — The static layout doesn’t fully demonstrate how AEO interrelates with other services. The page could better visualise the question → answer process and emphasise authority building. Without proof points, the promised metrics may seem aspirational.  
    
* **Next Step** — Create an engaging page that shows the lifecycle of questions across search engines and AI models. Use interactive graphics to explain research, answer writing, formatting and monitoring. Provide examples and call out benefits.

### **1\. Core Content Audit**

1. **Hero** — Title *“Answer engine optimisation (AEO)”* and subheading about getting featured in snippets, voice search and AI responses. CTAs offer a question analysis and link to the parent service.  
     
2. **Why AEO Matters** — Six feature points: Conversational content optimisation, Featured snippet optimisation, Question‑answer mapping, Authority building, Zero‑click optimisation, Multi‑platform coverage[\[3\]](https://lightspeedwp.figma.site/services/ai/answer-engine-optimisation).  
     
3. **Process** — Four stages with bullet lists:  
     
4. **Question Research** — Question inventory, search intent mapping, competitor question analysis, priority matrix.  
     
5. **Answer Development** — Direct answer creation, long‑form content development, FAQ schema implementation, answer structure optimisation[\[4\]](https://lightspeedwp.figma.site/services/ai/answer-engine-optimisation).  
     
6. **Format Optimisation** — Featured snippet formatting, People Also Ask optimisation, voice search optimisation, conversational tone adaptation[\[1\]](https://lightspeedwp.figma.site/services/ai/answer-engine-optimisation).  
     
7. **Monitoring & Refinement** — Answer visibility tracking, featured snippet monitoring, voice search performance, monthly optimisation reports[\[1\]](https://lightspeedwp.figma.site/services/ai/answer-engine-optimisation).  
     
8. **Expected Results** — Four metrics: 400% increase in featured snippets, 3× more voice search visibility, 85% answer accuracy rate, 2–4 months time to first results[\[2\]](https://lightspeedwp.figma.site/services/ai/answer-engine-optimisation).  
     
9. **CTA** — Invitation to request a free question analysis.

### **2\. Recommended WebGL & Interaction Plan**

1. **Q\&A Constellation Hero** — Use WebGL to depict a constellation of question marks that animate into answer bubbles. Each question mark twinkles, then morphs into a floating answer. When hovering, the answer expands to show a sample snippet or voice response. Include a gentle flow of particles to symbolise information exchange.  
     
2. **Question Research Visualiser** — Create an interactive word cloud or cluster diagram. Users can input a keyword (or select from examples) and watch relevant question topics cluster together. Lines connect similar questions, illustrating how LightSpeed identifies patterns.  
     
3. **Process Timeline Animation** — Build an animated path where a single question travels through the four stages (Research → Development → Format → Monitoring). At each stage, icons and bullet points appear. The path glows brighter as the question transforms into an authoritative answer.

### **3\. Content Enhancement Actions**

1. **Illustrate Authority Building** — Provide examples of authoritative content improvements (e.g., adding credentials to authors, citing research). Link to the AI engine optimisation page to explain how E‑E‑A‑T signals complement AEO.  
     
2. **Add Proof & Case Studies** — Include a mini case study demonstrating a 400% increase in snippets or 3× voice visibility. Use screenshots or transcripts to build credibility.  
     
3. **Explain Multi‑Platform Coverage** — Clarify which platforms are covered (e.g., Google’s Answer Box, Bing’s AI responses, Alexa/Google Assistant). Use logos and short descriptions.

### **4\. Notes & Considerations**

* Use neon cyan, lime and purple to differentiate this page while staying within the AI identity.  
    
* Provide alt text and accessible labels for Q\&A animations. Offer a pause or static mode for reduced motion preferences.  
    
* Encourage cross‑linking to the AI search visibility and engine optimisation pages to show the broader strategy.
