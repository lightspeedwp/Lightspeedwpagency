# **AI Content Generation Page Brief**

### **🔹 Summary**

* **Value** — The AI Content Generation page presents LightSpeed’s capability to produce on‑brand content at scale, highlighting huge gains in content output, time savings, brand consistency and multi‑language coverage[\[1\]](https://lightspeedwp.figma.site/solutions/ai/content-generation). It showcases features like blog automation, product descriptions and AI image generation[\[2\]](https://lightspeedwp.figma.site/solutions/ai/content-generation).  
    
* **Risks** — Without dynamic visuals, the concept of AI‑generated content feels abstract. The pricing table currently distracts from the benefits and may not align with your future pricing strategy. The page could better connect to audience‑specific benefits.  
    
* **Next Step** — Build a lively, interactive page that visualises content generation as flowing text and images. Remove pricing tables and instead emphasise results and case studies. Provide clear CTA to request an AI content audit.

### **1\. Core Content Audit**

1. **Hero** — Title *“Content Generation AI”* in neon pink, with a subheading about generating on‑brand content at scale[\[3\]](https://lightspeedwp.figma.site/solutions/ai/content-generation).  
     
2. **Impact Metrics** — Four boxes quantify the impact: *Produce 10x more content*, *Time reduction*, *Brand accuracy*, *Languages*[\[1\]](https://lightspeedwp.figma.site/solutions/ai/content-generation).  
     
3. **Capabilities** — Six features: Blog post automation, Product descriptions, Multi‑language support, AI image generation, Content refresh, Plagiarism & fact check[\[2\]](https://lightspeedwp.figma.site/solutions/ai/content-generation).  
     
4. **Who Benefits?** — Four audience categories: E‑Commerce Stores, Content publishers, Marketing agencies, Multi‑Language sites. Each lists industries and bullet benefits (e.g., more product pages, faster editorial cycles, multilingual support)[\[4\]\[5\]](https://lightspeedwp.figma.site/solutions/ai/content-generation).  
     
5. **Pricing & FAQ** — Pricing tiers (Starter, Professional, Enterprise) and FAQs (SEO impact, brand voice matching, integration, human review)[\[6\]](https://lightspeedwp.figma.site/solutions/ai/content-generation).

### **2\. Recommended WebGL & Interaction Plan**

1. **Flowing Text Hero** — Use WebGL to create a “river” of 3D text and icons that flows horizontally across the hero. The text is drawn from real blog titles, product descriptions or keywords. Neon pink particles swirl around the words, emphasising the creative energy of AI content generation. Interact by hovering to pause the flow or highlight certain words.  
     
2. **Metric Counters** — Replace static metric boxes with animated counters that spin up to their final values. When each counter completes, a small burst of particles radiates outward (with reduced‑motion fallback to static numbers).  
     
3. **Audience Carousel** — Convert the audience categories into a carousel of 3D cards. Each card flips on hover to reveal bullet benefits and a relevant icon or mini‑illustration (e.g., a storefront for e‑commerce, a magazine for publishers).  
     
4. **Feature Icons** — For each capability, introduce small interactive 3D icons (e.g., a pencil for blog automation, a shopping cart for product descriptions). These icons rotate gently and change colour on hover.

### **3\. Content Enhancement Actions**

1. **Remove Pricing Tables** — Since the future site will not display pricing, eliminate the pricing section. Instead, highlight measurable outcomes (e.g., “Publish 10 articles in 1 hour”) and direct visitors to request a customised quote.  
     
2. **Reposition FAQs** — Integrate frequently asked questions into an accordion at the bottom of the page. Add examples of successful content programmes and guidelines on human review processes.  
     
3. **Call to Action** — Add a prominent CTA inviting visitors to request a content generation audit or demo. Reinforce trust by mentioning data privacy and human oversight.

### **4\. Notes & Considerations**

* Use the neon pink and cyan colour palette to differentiate this page from other AI services while maintaining brand continuity.  
    
* Ensure text remains legible against dynamic backgrounds; use semi‑transparent panels where necessary.  
    
* Provide examples of multilingual output and brand style matching to demonstrate the system’s sophistication.
