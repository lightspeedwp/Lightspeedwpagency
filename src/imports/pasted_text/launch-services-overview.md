# **Services – Launch Stage**

### **Summary**

* **Value** – Prepares the website for the world. The launch stage provisions production hosting, performs final QA, trains clients and ensures a smooth, zero‑downtime go‑live. It marks the hand‑off from development to operation.  
    
* **Risks** – If done hastily, launch can introduce downtime, broken integrations or poor first impressions. Missing SSL or caching configuration hurts performance and security. Without training, clients may struggle to maintain their site.  
    
* **Next step** – Update messaging to highlight how AI tools assist in QA, launch automation and training. Incorporate WebGL elements that visualise the transition from staging to production.

### **Content audit**

* **Hero messaging** – The page opens with **“Launch. Deploy. Train. Go live.”** and describes provisioning production hosting, running QA checks and flipping the switch, with a typical duration of **1–2 weeks**[\[1\]](https://lightspeedwp.figma.site/services/launch). The hero should emphasise AI‑enabled launch management and training automation.  
    
* **Deliverables** – The “What you’ll achieve in Launch” section lists: provisioned production hosting, DNS cutover with zero‑downtime strategy, SSL/CDN caching configured[\[2\]](https://lightspeedwp.figma.site/services/launch). Add deliverables like **AI‑assisted QA report**, **automated backup configuration** and **performance benchmarking**.  
    
* **Services in this phase** – Currently shows **Hosting & Support** and **Training**[\[3\]](https://lightspeedwp.figma.site/services/launch). Clarify how these services leverage AI: automated scaling, anomaly detection, AI‑generated documentation and on‑demand chat support.  
    
* **What you’ll receive** – The page details a production environment (optimised server with staging & production), launch checklist (50‑point QA covering SEO, accessibility, performance, security), training documentation (custom video guides and SOPs), and a go‑live report (post‑launch metrics)[\[4\]](https://lightspeedwp.figma.site/services/launch). Ensure these items reflect AI‑integrated tooling – e.g., AI‑generated training videos, dynamic go‑live dashboards.  
    
* **Client role** – Includes final content review, attending training sessions, coordinating DNS changes and announcing the launch[\[4\]](https://lightspeedwp.figma.site/services/launch). Suggest adding responsibilities like reviewing automated QA results and verifying AI integrations.  
    
* **Testimonials and CTA** – A testimonial describes a calm launch with effective training[\[5\]](https://lightspeedwp.figma.site/services/launch). The CTA encourages planning your launch with a bullet list of deliverables and a “Get Launch‑Ready” button[\[6\]](https://lightspeedwp.figma.site/services/launch). Update to emphasise AI‑powered stability and confidence.

### **WebGL hero & interactive ideas**

* **Hero concept** – Visualise the site launch as a rocket taking off from a launch pad made of WordPress blocks. The rocket is built from neon‑cyan and neon‑yellow geometric shapes (representing the site) that stack and ignite. A particle trail follows the rocket into the sky, symbolising the move from staging to production. On hover, the rocket tilts toward the cursor, emphasising control and stability.  
    
* **Launch timeline** – Create an interactive timeline that animates through the final QA checklist, DNS cutover and production activation. Each milestone lights up as the user scrolls, with tooltips describing tasks (e.g., “Run accessibility audits”, “Configure CDN caching”).  
    
* **Training module** – Offer a modal or slide‑out panel with short video snippets or interactive AI chat that demonstrates how to update content, manage AI integrations and monitor performance. Use WebGL to animate icons representing videos and chat bubbles.

### **Recommended actions**

1. **Refine copy** to highlight AI‑assisted launch operations: automated QA, predictive scaling, real‑time monitoring and AI‑generated training materials.  
     
2. **Present deliverables** in a clear checklist covering production environment, QA, training docs, go‑live report, AI audit and performance benchmarks. Emphasise zero‑downtime migration and security hardening.  
     
3. **Explain client responsibilities** succinctly: final content check, attendance at training, DNS coordination and sign‑off on AI integration tests. Provide an onboarding checklist.  
     
4. **Add a case study** showing how AI automation reduced launch risk and improved time to go live.  
     
5. **CTA** – Encourage visitors to book a launch consultation. Use dynamic hover effects on the button (e.g., rocket flame animations) and reinforce the message of a calm, AI‑guided launch.
