# Getting Started with LightSpeed

### Summary

* **Value** – Provides clients with a clear roadmap for beginning a project, listing what materials and access are required and explaining how the project will progress. It sets expectations and helps clients prepare efficiently.

* **Risks** – If instructions are unclear or outdated, clients may delay project kick‑off or send incomplete information. The guide must reflect current processes and AI integrations.

* **Next step** – Update the Getting Started guide to align with the six‑stage lifecycle (Discover → Create → Build → Launch → Grow → Evolve) and integrate AI‑specific preparation steps.

### Content audit

* **Materials collection** – The current guide instructs clients to gather content (copy, images, logos) and upload via Google Drive using a specified folder structure and naming convention[\[1\]](https://lightspeedwp.agency/getting-started-with-lightspeed/#:~:text=What%20We%20Need%20to%20Get,Started). Keep this guidance but add a section on **AI prompt guidelines**: provide examples of tone and style for AI‑generated copy, supply sample Q\&As for answer engine content and share brand voice notes for AI chatbots.

* **Brand assets** – Clients are asked to provide logo files, brand guidelines and style guides. Emphasise the importance of high‑resolution logos, design tokens and component libraries for AI‑driven automation.

* **Access & logins** – The guide requests hosting credentials, WordPress admin access and third‑party service logins[\[1\]](https://lightspeedwp.agency/getting-started-with-lightspeed/#:~:text=What%20We%20Need%20to%20Get,Started). Update to include credentials for AI tools, analytics platforms, SEO/AEO services and any content APIs.

* **Process overview** – Describes a 2025 web design workflow: discovery workshop, Figma design system, prototype, staging website build, feedback & QA, launch, post‑launch support[\[1\]](https://lightspeedwp.agency/getting-started-with-lightspeed/#:~:text=What%20We%20Need%20to%20Get,Started). Replace with the new six‑stage cycle and explain AI involvement at each phase (e.g., AI content generation in Create, AI analytics in Grow).

* **Communication & tools** – Lists tools such as Google Drive, Figma, BugHerd, Google Meet and email for collaboration[\[1\]](https://lightspeedwp.agency/getting-started-with-lightspeed/#:~:text=What%20We%20Need%20to%20Get,Started). Expand to include AI chatbots for support, project management integration (e.g., Asana with AI summaries) and guidelines for file organisation.

* **Payment structure** – Notes a standard deposit and milestone payment schedule. Ensure this information remains accurate and include any new payment models for AI subscription services.

* **Why this workflow works** – Explains that structured content, design tokens and clear approvals streamline projects[\[1\]](https://lightspeedwp.agency/getting-started-with-lightspeed/#:~:text=What%20We%20Need%20to%20Get,Started). Reinforce that the new AI‑enabled workflow reduces cycles, improves quality and provides measurable results.

### WebGL & interactive ideas

* **Interactive checklist** – Build a dynamic checklist where clients can tick off tasks as they gather materials. A 3D progress bar fills as tasks are completed. Provide tooltips with examples and links to templates (e.g., content spreadsheet, AI prompt brief).

* **Folder structure visualiser** – Offer a WebGL preview of the recommended folder structure. Users can click on each folder to see a description of the expected contents. The tree expands and collapses smoothly.

* **Process map** – Illustrate the six stages as an animated roadmap. When a stage is selected, the map zooms in to show required inputs, typical activities and AI touchpoints. This helps clients understand what happens and how to prepare.

### Recommended actions

1. **Align the guide with the new lifecycle** – Replace the old workflow steps with the six stages and update instructions accordingly.

2. **Add AI preparation guidance** – Explain what clients need to do to enable AI features (e.g., collect FAQs for AEO, provide brand voice guidelines for AI content tools, grant access to analytics). Offer template documents for prompts.

3. **Include a downloadable kit** – Provide templates for content inventories, image checklists, AI prompt briefs and access credentials forms.

4. **Visualise progress** – Use interactive checklists or progress bars to help clients track their readiness. Send automated reminders if tasks are incomplete.

5. **Offer support resources** – Link to support channels, knowledge base articles and training sessions to help clients understand each requirement.
